# Real-money go-live gates

Release decision: **NO-GO** until every applicable item has a named owner,
dated evidence, independent approval and expiry/review date.

## Legal and distribution

- Written product classification for every product and jurisdiction
- Required licences or executed agreements with appropriately licensed partners
- Approved customer, professional-client and country matrices
- Terms, privacy notice, risk disclosures and complaints/appeals process
- Apple and Google distribution approval for the exact enabled product set
- Explicit permanent mobile denial for any binary-option-like product

## Financial integrity

- Production PostgreSQL adapter proven under serializable concurrency
- Independently reviewed double-entry chart of accounts
- Customer/house money segregation and safeguarding model
- Daily bank, custody, wallet, collateral and ledger reconciliation
- Fee, treasury, reserve, chargeback and insolvency procedures
- Settlement replay proving exactly-once economic result

## Compliance and customer protection

- KYC/KYB, age, PEP, sanctions and source-of-funds providers
- AML scenarios, case management, MLRO escalation and regulatory reporting
- Travel Rule and self-hosted-wallet procedure where relevant
- Responsible-use controls, self-exclusion and Spelpaus where applicable
- Privacy impact assessment, retention schedule and data-subject workflows

## Security and resilience

- Independent penetration test with no open critical/high finding
- Threat model review, secure SDLC, SAST/DAST/dependency scan and SBOM
- KMS/HSM/MPC design, documented key ceremony and recovery rehearsal
- Staff MFA, JIT access, session recording and quarterly access review
- Tested incident, business continuity, backup restore and regional failover
- Signed immutable builds, canary/rollback and controlled matching-engine upgrade
- DORA programme and ICT supplier register where applicable

## Operations

- 24/7 security, wallet, market, resolution and withdrawal on-call
- Runbooks tested in tabletop and technical exercises
- RPO/RTO measured, not assumed
- Price-feed licences permit display, calculation, settlement and retention
- Degraded feed, outlier, stale data, circuit-breaker and void drills passed
